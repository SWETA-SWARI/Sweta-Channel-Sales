  • Valid RERA licence is mandatory: no commission invoice and no new enrolment without it.
    • 
      
  • Invoice only after 20% payment + sale agreement execution.
    •

    •

    •

    •

    • 
      
  • Clash policy: first lead date and time wins; if tied, first interaction (over 1 minute) wins.
    • 
      
  • No kickbacks to customers. Referral incentives only up to Alcove's referral scheme value. Campaigns need prior approval. CPs introduce themselves as Authorised Channel Partners, not Alcove staff.

URO playbook · "Own with Confidence"

      
Most NK buyers are 2nd-home buyers. Deals die after the family visit, before token. All 5 conditions must be present: Desire · Conviction · Financial readiness · Alignment · Safety.

The 5 hidden fears
    
        
  • Regret: what if this is a mistake?
  • Liquidity trap: money locked if an emergency hits.
    • 
        
  • Social proof gap: resale by existing owners is cheaper.
  • Commitment weight: token is the gateway to 50–60L + EMI.
    • 
        
  • Inertia: walking away costs nothing today.

      
Weekly rhythm & warm restart
    
        
  • Fri–Sun: execution (site visits, family visits, token talks). Mon: data review. Tue: learning. Wed: weekend prep. Thu: confirm weekend appointments.
    • 
        
  • Pipeline buckets: Hot (stalled after family visit, reason maps to E1–E4) · Warm (visited, reason unclear) · Cold–financial (E2A/E3/E6) · Dead.
    • 
        
  • Log every visit the same evening: prospect, visit type, elements used, outcome, obstacle, missing condition, next action.
